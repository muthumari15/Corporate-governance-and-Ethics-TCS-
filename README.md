# Corporate Governance and Ethics (TCS)

This case study focuses on Corporate Governance and Ethics at Tata Consultancy Services (TCS). It explains how the company follows ethical business practices, maintains transparency, protects stakeholders, and ensures accountability. It also covers the key policies, strengths, areas for improvement, and recommendations to improve corporate governance and ethical behaviour. This study helped me understand the importance of honesty, responsibility, and trust in business.
